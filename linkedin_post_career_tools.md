**Learning by building 🚀**

I've been exploring agentic AI by building NovusOrbit—a side project that applies AI agents to career guidance. Instead of one generic chatbot, it uses specialized AI agents working together:

- A career counselor who knows your history
- A writing coach for your resume
- An interview specialist with your achievements
- A job matcher aligned with your goals



**What you can do:**

💬 Get career advice based on YOUR actual experience  
📝 Transform resume sections into compelling stories  
💼 Prep for interviews with personalized questions & answers  
🎯 Find jobs that match your skills (and see what to learn next)

**Inspired by:** Ed Donner's Agentic AI courses—applying the same principles that make AI tutoring effective to career development.

**More features coming soon:** Resume scoring, personalized learning paths, and career trajectory planning 😀
