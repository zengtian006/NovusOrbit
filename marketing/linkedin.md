> What I learned turning an open-source AI tutor into a career assistant:
> 1. Retrieval over your CV matters more than the model. Generic answers came from missing context, not a weak LLM.
> 2. Job boards change their HTML constantly. Scrapers need fallbacks.
> 3. Multi-user data isolation should be designed in from day one.

**2. A useful tip (link to a guide in the comments)**
> Most behavioral interview answers fail the same way: 80% context, 0% result.
> The STAR method fixes that: Situation (one sentence), Task, Action (the longest part, and say "I", not "we"), Result (with a number).
> I wrote a full guide with a worked example. Link in the comments.
