## Copy to paste

**Name:** NovusOrbit
**URL:** https://www.novusorbit.com
**Category:** AI tools → Career / Job search / Productivity
**Pricing:** Free
**Tags:** ai, career, job search, interview preparation, resume, open source

**Short description (≤ 100 characters):**
Free AI career assistant: interview prep, resume rewrites and job matches personalized from your CV.

**Medium description (~ 300 characters):**
NovusOrbit is a free AI career assistant. Upload your CV once, then get interview questions and personalized answers for the job you want, AI resume rewrites with PDF export, ranked job matches from Indeed and LinkedIn, and a tracker for every application. Open source, in English and Chinese.

**Long description:**
Job hunting usually means re-explaining your background to every tool. NovusOrbit reads your CV and documents once and uses them everywhere:
- Interview prep: questions generated from the job description at the difficulty you choose, with suggested answers built from your own experience.
- Job suggestions: live listings from Indeed and LinkedIn, ranked against your CV, with the reasons for each match and the gaps to close.
- Resume writer: rewrite, shorten or expand any bullet in place, then export to Markdown or PDF.
- Job tracker: follow every application from interested to offered.
- Career chat: ask questions answered from your CV, with optional web search.
Free to use with Google sign-in. Open source under AGPL-3.0.

**Images:** `web/app/opengraph-image` (1200×630; download it from https://www.novusorbit.com/opengraph-image), plus the screenshots in `assets/screenshots/`. **Logo:** `web/public/icon-512.png`.
