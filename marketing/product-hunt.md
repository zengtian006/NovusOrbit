## Maker's first comment

> Hey Product Hunt 👋
>
> I'm Tim, and I built NovusOrbit after my own job search turned into a mess of browser tabs, spreadsheets and pasting my CV into ChatGPT over and over.
>
> The idea is simple: **upload your CV once, and every tool already knows you.**
>
> 🎯 **Interview prep**: pick a job you saved and a difficulty. You get the questions you're likely to face and suggested answers that use your real projects.
> 📝 **Resume writer**: select any bullet and rewrite, shorten or expand it. Export to PDF.
> 🔎 **Job suggestions**: it searches live listings on Indeed and LinkedIn, ranks them against your CV, and explains each match and the gaps.
> 📋 **Job tracker**: every application from "interested" to "offered".
>
> It's free and open source (AGPL).
>
> I'd really value your honest feedback: what would make this the first tool you open when you start job hunting?

## Gallery

Product Hunt shows images at 1270×760. Capture these signed in, with realistic demo data (use a sample CV, not your own personal details):

1. **Hero:** `web/app/opengraph-image` card, or the home screen with a chat answer that quotes the CV.
2. **Interview prep:** generated questions for a real-looking job, with one answer expanded.
3. **Job suggestions:** the ranked list with match explanations visible.
4. **Resume writer:** a before/after bullet rewrite.
5. **Job tracker:** a board with a few jobs at different stages.
6. **Video (optional, strongly recommended):** 30 to 60 seconds: upload CV → save job → interview prep → job suggestions. Loom or QuickTime screen recording is enough.

Public-page screenshots already captured in `assets/screenshots/` can fill gaps.
