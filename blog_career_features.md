## The Tools That Do the Heavy Lifting

### 1. Your AI Career Coach (Chat)

Upload your resume and certificates, then chat with the AI about anything career-related. It's like texting a friend who happens to have read your entire work history.

Ask things like:
- "What should I highlight in my resume for this product manager role?"
- "How do I negotiate a higher salary?"
- "What's missing from my profile to get that promotion?"

The AI doesn't give generic advice—it references your actual experience and suggests next steps that make sense for where you are right now.

### 2. Smart Resume Editor

Tired of staring at your resume wondering how to make it better? This tool does three things:

**Rewrite** - Make bullet points sound more professional and impactful
**Shorten** - Fit more into less space without losing the good stuff  
**Expand** - Turn brief descriptions into compelling stories

Here's a real example:
- Before: "Managed software development projects"
- After: "Led cross-functional teams delivering product features that improved sprint velocity by 40% while maintaining 98% on-time delivery"

The AI can even remember details from other parts of your resume and weave them in naturally.

### 3. Interview Prep Assistant

Found a job you want? Drop the job description in and get:
- Custom interview questions (easy, medium, or hard difficulty)
- Suggested answers using your real experience
- Practice material that's actually relevant

No more generic "tell me about yourself" prep. You get questions specific to that role and answers that showcase what you've actually done.

### 4. Job Matcher

Not sure what jobs to apply for? This tool analyzes your background and suggests roles that fit. For each suggestion, you get:
- A match score
- Skills you already have for this job
- Skills you'd need to learn
- How this fits your career path

You can filter by location, industry, experience level, and role type.

## The Secret Sauce: Agentic AI Framework

So what makes NovusOrbit different from other AI tools? It's all about the architecture.

Most AI career tools are just chatbots with generic prompts. NovusOrbit uses something called an **agentic AI system**—basically, specialized AI agents that work together, each with its own expertise.

Think of it like a career services office where you have:
- A **Career Counselor** (Chat Agent) who knows your entire background
- A **Writing Coach** (Edit Agent) who polishes your documents
- An **Interview Prep Specialist** (Interview Agent) who simulates real interviews
- A **Job Matchmaker** (Job Suggest Agent) who finds opportunities

These agents don't just respond to your questions—they actively use tools to help you. They can search through your portfolio, look up current industry trends, analyze job descriptions, and pull together information from multiple sources. All working behind the scenes to give you personalized advice.
